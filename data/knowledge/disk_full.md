# Disk Full

A server or container filesystem has insufficient free space.

Common Causes:
- Excessive logs
- Large temporary files
- Unused artifacts
- Database growth

Evidence:
- No space left on device
- Disk full
- Filesystem full

Diagnostics:
- Check disk usage
- Identify large files
- Review log retention

Remediation:
Free disk space or increase storage.

Prevention:
Disk monitoring and log rotation.
